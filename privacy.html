Information we collect

MINAMI STOCK is a site for stock photos, AI and everyday tools, and an affiliate shop. We collect only what we need to run these features.

Account details

When you register or sign in, we store your name, email address and a unique account ID. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, so we never see or store your password in plain text.

Content you add

  • Products you post to the shop: name, price, description, link and image.
  • Uploads you send through the Upload page, with their titles and keywords.
  • Generated images from the AI generator, saved to your own account so you can view them again.
  • Seller dashboard data tied to your account, such as the products you manage.

Automatic data

Like most websites, our servers and hosting provider log standard technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, internet service provider, date and time of visit, referring and exit pages, and the pages or buttons you click. This is used for security and to understand how the site is used. It is not used to identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show your products, uploads and generated images to you, and to others where you chose to publish them.
  • To run, protect and improve the site, including fixing errors and blocking abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To show ads and measure how affiliate links perform.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

We and our partners use cookies and similar browser storage to remember your sign-in, keep your preferences, and understand which pages are visited. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some parts of the site, such as staying signed in, may stop working.

Third-party services

Some features rely on outside services. Each one has its own privacy policy that applies to the data it handles.

Google Firebase

Authentication, Firestore and the Realtime Database are provided by Google Firebase. Your account details and the content you add are stored there.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or through the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

Other advertisers may also use cookies and web beacons. Each advertising partner has its own policy on user data, and we do not control it.

Image hosting and fonts

Product photos and some site assets load from external hosts, such as image hosting services and content delivery networks. These hosts can see your IP address when your browser requests the files.

Affiliate links

Products in our shop link to outside stores. If you buy through one of these links, the store may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Once you leave MINAMI STOCK, the store's own privacy policy applies to your purchase and your data.

Who can see your data

  • Public by design: products you post to the shop and uploads you publish can be seen by any visitor.
  • Private to you: your email address, account details and saved generated images are not shown to other users.
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase, AdSense) and our hosting provider process data on our behalf to operate the site.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our users and the site from fraud or abuse.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the content linked to it at any time using the contact details below. Some log data may remain for a short period in backups or provider logs before it is removed.

Security

We use HTTPS and Firebase security rules so that each account can only access its own private data. No website can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong, unique password and do not share it.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • Edit or remove products and uploads you posted from your dashboard.
  • Block cookies in your browser, or opt out of personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings.
  • Stop using the site and ask us to close your account.

Children

MINAMI STOCK is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed. By continuing to use the site after an update, you accept the new version.
