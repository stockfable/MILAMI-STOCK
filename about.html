Our story

MINAMI STOCK began with a simple question: why should great creative tools and a global marketplace feel out of reach for people who live far from big cities?

Muhammad Mahmudul Hasan started the platform from a village in Bangladesh, with a phone, a lot of curiosity and the belief that talent is spread evenly across the world while opportunity is not. He taught himself to design and build for the web, and he wanted to create the kind of place he had been looking for: one where photographers, designers and small sellers could share their work, and where everyday tools such as image converters and PDF makers were free, fast and easy to use.

Today MINAMI STOCK brings those ideas together in one place. You can browse and license stock photos, use AI tools to generate and improve images, shop trusted products, and open your own seller account to start earning from what you create.

Great work can come from anywhere. We build the tools and the stage, creators bring the rest. Muhammad Mahmudul Hasan, Founder

Frequently asked questions

What is MINAMI STOCK?

MINAMI STOCK is a creative platform with stock photos, AI image tools, free everyday tools and a marketplace where sellers can post their own products.

Who runs the site?

MINAMI STOCK was created and is run by Muhammad Mahmudul Hasan from Bangladesh.

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. The free tools such as Text to PDF, QR Code and Word Counter work without signing in. An account is needed to save your AI creations, upload content or sell products.

How do I become a seller?

Create an account, open your dashboard and add your first product with a photo, a title and a price. Your listing appears in the shop once it is posted.

How can I contact you?

Use the links in the footer of the home page, or write to the founder through the contact details listed on the site.
