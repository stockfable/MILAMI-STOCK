Upload Guidelines & Conditions

Please read the following rules carefully before uploading your content to MINAMI STOCK.

Technical Requirements

  • File Size: Minimum size is 2 MB. Files smaller than this will be automatically rejected.
  • Resolution: Minimum dimensions must be 2000 x 2000 pixels (both Width and Height).
  • Format: Only JPG, PNG, and WebP formats are accepted.

Metadata & Keywords

  • Title: A clear, descriptive title is mandatory for every upload.
  • Category: You must select the most relevant category from the dropdown.
  • Keywords: Provide between 5 and 50 relevant keywords. Separate them with commas.

Content Rules

  • AI Generated: If your image is created by AI tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, etc.), you must check the "AI Generated" box.
  • People & Property: If a recognizable person or private property is in the image, you must attach a valid Release Document.

Copyright & Legal

  • Ownership: You must own the full commercial rights to the image.
  • Strict Policy: Uploading copyrighted content, stolen images, or illegal material will result in an immediate account ban.

By clicking "I Understand & Agree", you confirm that your uploads comply with all the rules above.
