❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Img.Upscaler?
Img.Upscaler is a 100% free AI-powered image upscaling tool that runs directly in your browser. It enlarges images by 2x or 4x while improving clarity, sharpness, and overall quality — no sign-up required.
Is it really free to use?
Yes, absolutely. Img.Upscaler is completely free with no hidden costs, no subscriptions, and no limits. All processing happens directly on your device.
Are my images safe and private?
Yes, 100%. Your images never leave your device. All upscaling is done locally in your browser, so nothing is uploaded to any server.
What image formats are supported?
You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP, and most other common image formats. The upscaled result is saved as a high-quality JPEG file.
What's the difference between 2x and 4x upscaling?
2x doubles the image dimensions (500×500 → 1000×1000) and is ideal for web use. 4x quadruples them (500×500 → 2000×2000) and is better for printing, large displays, or detailed zooming.
Can I upscale multiple images at once?
Currently, Img.Upscaler processes one image at a time to ensure the highest quality output. Simply upload the next image after downloading the previous one.
