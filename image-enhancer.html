Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our upscaler.

How does the upscaler work?

Our engine uses a smart interpolation algorithm that analyzes the original pixels and intelligently adds new pixel data. This makes the image bigger without the blurry look of traditional scaling.

Will the quality suffer?

No. Our upscaler preserves detail by keeping edges sharp and textures natural. You get a larger image that looks crisp and clear — not blurry.

Can I upscale by 4x?

Yes. You can enlarge images by 2x or 4x. Just upload your photo and use the download button to save the upscaled version.

Is the tool free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no watermark, no limits. Download as many upscaled images as you want.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device — 100% private and secure.
