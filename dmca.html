DMCA Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026 6 min read Copyright compliance
⚠️ Copyright Infringement Notice MINAMI STOCK respects the intellectual property rights of others. If you believe your copyrighted work has been used on our site without authorization, please follow the procedure below to file a DMCA takedown notice.

1. Our Commitment

MINAMI STOCK (the "Site") operates in compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and similar copyright laws worldwide. We take copyright infringement seriously and will respond promptly to valid notices of claimed infringement.

Our policy is to:

  • Respond to all valid DMCA notices within 48–72 hours
  • Remove or disable access to allegedly infringing content
  • Terminate accounts of repeat infringers
  • Cooperate with copyright holders to resolve disputes

2. How to File a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner, or authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that content on our Site infringes your copyright, you may submit a written notification containing the following information:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work — Describe the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed, or provide a representative list if multiple works.
  2. Identification of the infringing material — Provide the exact URL(s) of the content on our Site that you claim is infringing.
  3. Your contact information — Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. Good faith statement — A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Accuracy statement under penalty of perjury — A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your signature — A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized person.
Note: Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be subject to liability for damages.

3. Submit Your Notice

You can file a DMCA notice using one of the following methods:

📧 Email (fastest)

Send your complete DMCA notice to: dmca@minamistock.com

Subject line: DMCA Takedown Request - [Your Name]

📝 Or use this form

Provide URLs to your original content if possible

4. What Happens Next

After we receive your valid DMCA notice:

  1. Within 48 hours — We acknowledge receipt of your notice
  2. Within 72 hours — We review the claim and, if valid, remove or disable access to the content
  3. Notification — We notify the uploader of the removal and provide them a copy of your notice
  4. Counter-notice window — The uploader has 10 business days to file a counter-notice

5. Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice containing:

  • Your name, address, phone number, and email
  • Identification of the removed content and its previous location
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the content was removed by mistake
  • A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of a federal court
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Send counter-notices to: dmca@minamistock.com

6. Repeat Infringers

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, MINAMI STOCK will terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers of copyright.

7. Our Content Sources

  • Public domain collections — Photos with expired copyright
  • User uploads — Verified by uploaders who confirm ownership

If you find that any content on our site infringes your copyright, please notify us using the procedure above so we can take appropriate action.
