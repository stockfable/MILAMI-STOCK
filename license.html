License summary

All photos on MINAMI STOCK are provided under a simple, permissive license inspired by Pixabay's own license. In plain English:

  • Free to use — for personal and commercial projects
  • No attribution — you don't need to credit anyone
  • Modify freely — crop, edit, combine with other content
  • Don't resell — you can't sell the photo itself as a product
  • Don't redistribute — don't upload to another stock site
  • Don't train AI — no ML/AI model training without permission

Allowed uses (Standard License)

You may use MINAMI STOCK photos for:

  • Websites, blogs, and landing pages
  • Social media content (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube)
  • Marketing materials, ads, and email campaigns
  • Presentations and business documents
  • Print materials (posters, flyers, brochures, magazines)
  • Book covers and illustrations
  • School and university projects
  • Personal use (wallpapers, greeting cards, art projects)
  • Commercial products where the photo is not the main product

Not allowed

  • Reselling the photo as-is — you can't download a photo and sell it on another stock photo site
  • Redistributing as a collection — don't bundle our photos and share them as a pack
  • Using to imply endorsement — don't use a photo of a person or brand in a way that suggests they endorse your product
  • AI training — training machine learning models on our collection is prohibited
  • Illegal or harmful use — no defamatory, obscene, or illegal content
Note about people and brands: Photos may show recognizable people or branded products. You are responsible for obtaining any model releases or property releases needed for your specific use, especially in advertising.

Quick answers

Do I need to credit MINAMI STOCK?
No. Attribution is not required. But we always appreciate a link back if you want to support us.
Can I use photos in YouTube videos and monetize them?
Yes. Standard License covers YouTube and other monetized platforms. No additional license needed.
Can I sell products with these photos on them?
Yes, for standard products like posters, calendars, and prints. For merchandise like T-shirts and mugs where the photo is a primary product feature, an Extended License is recommended.
Can I use a photo on my company's website?
Yes. The Standard License covers commercial use on websites, including company sites, e-commerce, and SaaS products.
What if the photo shows a brand logo?
The photo itself is free to use, but brands and trademarks within the image may be protected. Don't use the photo in a way that suggests brand endorsement, and avoid using brand logos in commercial products without permission.
Can I use a photo of a person for advertising?
If the person is recognizable, you may need a model release for commercial advertising. For editorial use, no release is needed. When in doubt, choose photos where people aren't identifiable.
Do I need a license for each download?
No. One download = one license for that photo. You can download and use each photo as many times as you want.
Are the photos safe for kids' projects?
Yes. All photos on MINAMI STOCK are family-friendly and moderated. No inappropriate content is allowed.
